Naruto Shippuden Part 10: Seasons 16-17 (Episodes 349-372) - Every Episode Explained

Anime20 October 202616 min read

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This is Part 10 of the complete Naruto Shippuden episode-by-episode guide, covering Seasons 16 and 17 - Episodes 349 to 372. It is two things at once: a 13-episode anime-original flashback about Kakashi's years in the ANBU, and then the war's biggest lore reveal as Sasuke learns the truth about the village.

At a glance

  • Episodes: 349-372 (24 episodes)
  • Season 16: Kakashi: Shadow of the ANBU Black Ops - episodes 349-361 - anime-original
  • Season 17: The Fourth Great Ninja War: The Return of Team 7 - episodes 362-372 - manga canon
  • Aired: 6 February 2014 - 14 August 2014
  • Season 16 score: 7.5/10  ·  Season 17 score: 9/10

The Kakashi ANBU arc is filler, but it is filler with a purpose - it fills in the gap between Minato's death and the start of the original series, and it ends exactly where Naruto begins. Season 17 then returns to the war and delivers the biggest information dump in the franchise.

Every episode explained

Episode 349: The Mask That Hides the Heart

The Kakashi ANBU arc begins, set after Minato's death as Kakashi joins the ANBU Black Ops.

Note: A slower, darker change of pace.

Episode 350: Minato's Death

The aftermath of Minato and Kushina's deaths is shown, and how it shaped Konoha.

Note: Bleak, and well done.

Episode 351: Hashirama's Cells

The history of Hashirama's cells and the experiments around them is told.

Note: Ties together a lot of lore.

Episode 352: The Rogue Ninja Orochimaru

Orochimaru's fall from Konoha shinobi to rogue ninja is shown.

Note: A good origin for a great villain.

Episode 353: Orochimaru's Test Subject

Orochimaru's experiments are explored, and how far he had already gone.

Note: Dark.

Episode 354: Their Own Paths

Kakashi and his former teammates take different roads.

Note: Character work.

Episode 355: The Targeted Sharingan

The Sharingan becomes a target, and Kakashi is drawn into the politics of it.

Note: Plot-heavy.

Episode 356: A Shinobi of the Leaf

What it means to be a shinobi of the Leaf is examined through Kakashi's service.

Note: Thematic.

Episode 357: An Uchiha ANBU

Itachi joins the ANBU and works alongside Kakashi.

Note: A great pairing.

Episode 358: Coup d'Etat

The Uchiha clan's planned coup is set in motion.

Note: The tragedy starts to close in.

Episode 359: The Night of the Tragedy

The Uchiha massacre, from Kakashi's side of it.

Note: One of the heaviest episodes.

Episode 360: Jonin Leader

Kakashi becomes a jonin leader and the arc looks ahead to Team 7.

Note: A bridge to the main story.

Episode 361: Team 7

Team 7 is formed for the first time. The arc closes where the series began.

Note: A lovely full-circle ending.

Episode 362: Kakashi's Resolve

Back in the war, Kakashi finds his resolve and helps turn the fight against Obito.

Note: Strong return to canon.

Episode 363: The Allied Shinobi Forces Jutsu

The whole Alliance fights as one against the Ten-Tails.

Note: A huge team moment.

Episode 364: The Ties That Bind

The Ten-Tails transforms, and the Alliance suffers a devastating loss.

Note: One of the saddest episodes of the war.

Episode 365: Those Who Dance in the Shadows

Sasuke and Orochimaru move on Konoha while the war rages.

Note: Two plots at once.

Episode 366: The All-Knowing

Sasuke revives the previous Hokage and asks them the truth about the village.

Note: A huge lore episode.

Episode 367: Hashirama and Madara

Hashirama tells the story of how he and Madara met as children.

Note: Excellent backstory.

Episode 368: The Era of Warring States

The world before the villages is shown, and why the village system exists.

Note: Foundational lore.

Episode 369: My True Dream

Hashirama and Madara's shared dream of peace is told, and how it broke.

Note: Tragic.

Episode 370: Sasuke's Answer

Sasuke decides what he believes and what he will fight for.

Note: The point of the whole arc.

Episode 371: Hole

Sasuke reflects on the hole in his heart left by Itachi.

Note: Quiet, and effective.

Episode 372: Something to Fill the Hole

Sasuke decides what will fill it, and rejoins the war on his own terms.

Note: A satisfying turn.

Part 10 verdict

The Kakashi ANBU arc is the best filler stretch in Shippuden. It gives the Sharingan, Orochimaru's defection and the Uchiha massacre proper context, and ending it on the formation of Team 7 is a genuinely lovely touch. If you normally skip filler, this is the one to make an exception for.

Season 17 is essential. Episodes 366 to 372, where Sasuke revives the Hokage and learns why the village exists at all, are the philosophical heart of the entire series. Sasuke's decision at the end is the payoff the whole franchise was building toward.

Overall: 8.5/10. Great filler plus a canon season that explains everything.
