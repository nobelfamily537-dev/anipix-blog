Naruto Shippuden Part 13: Season 22 (Episodes 480-500) - Every Episode Explained

Anime20 October 202613 min read

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This is Part 13 - the final part of the complete Naruto Shippuden episode-by-episode guide, covering Season 22 - Episodes 480 to 500. After the war ended, Shippuden spent its last season on light novels: Sasuke's mission, Shikamaru's investigation, and finally Naruto's wedding.

At a glance

  • Episodes: 480-500 (21 episodes)
  • Season 22: Nostalgic Days / Sasuke Shinden / Shikamaru Hiden / Konoha Hiden - light novel adaptations
  • Aired: 20 October 2016 - 23 March 2017
  • Season 22 score: 7.5/10
  • Series status: complete at 500 episodes

This season is not action - it is an epilogue. It is about what everyone does once the fighting is over, and it ends with Naruto finally getting the ordinary life he spent the whole series fighting for. If you have come this far, you owe it to yourself to finish it.

Every episode explained

Episode 480: Naruto and Hinata

A gentle episode following a young Naruto and Hinata on the day they first met.

Note: A lovely, quiet opening to the final season.

Episode 481: Sasuke and Sakura

Sasuke and Sakura's early days are shown from a different angle.

Note: Character work.

Episode 482: Gaara and Shikamaru

Gaara's childhood and Shikamaru's school days are shown side by side.

Note: Makes you appreciate how far they came.

Episode 483: Jiraiya and Kakashi

Jiraiya's dreams and young Kakashi's loneliness are explored.

Note: Bittersweet, knowing what happens later.

Episode 484: Part 1: The Exploding Human

The Sasuke Shinden arc begins. Sasuke investigates a wave of exploding humans attacking villages.

Note: Sasuke's post-war work as a wandering protector.

Episode 485: Part 2: Coliseum

Sasuke travels to a coliseum where wealthy owners make shinobi fight for sport.

Note: Good worldbuilding.

Episode 486: Part 3: Fushin

The mystery deepens and a hidden kekkei genkai is revealed.

Note: Solid mystery.

Episode 487: Part 4: The Ketsuryugan

The Ketsuryugan, a rare eye technique, is explained along with a clan's tragedy.

Note: Great lore.

Episode 488: Part 5: The Last One

The arc concludes with Sasuke choosing to return to Konoha.

Note: A fitting close for Sasuke's redemption.

Episode 489: Part 1: The State of Affairs

The Shikamaru Hiden arc begins. Shikamaru works a desk job and investigates the Land of Silence.

Note: A quieter, more political story.

Episode 490: Part 2: Dark Clouds

Shikamaru's investigation deepens and the danger grows.

Note: Tense.

Episode 491: Part 3: Recklessness

Shikamaru takes a risky approach.

Note: Shows Shikamaru's flaws, which is good.

Episode 492: Part 4: Cloud of Suspicion

Suspicion spreads through the villages.

Note: Political intrigue.

Episode 493: Part 5: Dawn

The arc resolves, and Shikamaru makes peace with his role.

Note: A strong ending.

Episode 494: Part 1: Naruto's Wedding

The Konoha Hiden arc begins - everyone scrambles to find the perfect wedding gift for Naruto.

Note: Pure warmth.

Episode 495: Part 2: A Full-Powered Wedding Gift

Guy and Lee try to make the most over-the-top gift possible.

Note: Genuinely funny.

Episode 496: Part 3: Steam and Food Pills

A light episode about a hot spring and food pills.

Note: Comedy.

Episode 497: Part 4: The Kazekage's Wedding Gift

Gaara decides what to give Naruto.

Note: Sweet.

Episode 498: Part 5: The Last Mission

A secret mission becomes the framework for the final stretch.

Note: Sets up the ending.

Episode 499: Part 6: The Outcome of the Secret Mission

The mission's results are revealed, and everything comes together.

Note: Payoff.

Episode 500: Part 7: The Message

The series ends with Naruto's wedding and a message to everyone who watched.

Note: A perfect final episode.

Part 13 verdict

The first four episodes, which follow Naruto and Hinata, Sasuke and Sakura, Gaara and Shikamaru, and Jiraiya and Kakashi on ordinary days, are quietly the best thing here. They are not plot; they are payoffs for people you have watched grow up for 500 episodes.

The three novel arcs that follow are uneven but warm. Sasuke Shinden gives Sasuke a proper post-redemption role. Konoha Hiden, the wedding arc, is pure fan service in the best sense - it is a long goodbye to a cast the audience loves.

Overall: 7.5/10. A gentle, satisfying ending to a 500-episode journey.
